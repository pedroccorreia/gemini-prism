# Corporate Brand Guidelines (Fallback Template)

> **Usage Note:** Customize this template before running `make package-marketing` if your organization wants a tailored default Brand Kit baked into `brand-kit-governance.zip`. Live brand books or campaign briefs discovered in federated **Google Drive**, **Atlassian Confluence**, or **Slack** take precedence over these defaults.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

- **Brand Archetype:** Modern Enterprise Innovator — clear, credible, human-centered, and outcome-driven.
- **Target Audience:** Enterprise decision-makers, technical practitioners, and modern digital teams.
- **Value Promise:** Turning complex workflows into effortless, measurable business outcomes.

---

## 2. Voice & Copy Pillars

| Pillar | Do | Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clarity First** | Lead with concrete customer outcomes and active verbs. | Vague buzzwords, passive voice, or jargon stacks. |
| **Confident & Grounded** | Back claims with facts from approved product briefs. | Unverified superlatives (*"100% guaranteed"*, *"magic"*). |
| **Concise Scannability** | Keep social hooks under 12 words and CTAs to 2–4 action words. | Wall-of-text captions or multiple competing CTAs in one asset. |

---

## 3. Visual Palette & Generative Lighting Mapping

Generative image (**Imagen**) and video (**Veo**) models respond best when hex tokens are paired with tactile surface and lighting descriptions:

| Role | Token / Hex | Generative Prompt Descriptor (Imagen & Veo) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Canvas** | `#080D18` (Obsidian) | Deep midnight slate architectural environment, matte obsidian surfaces, clean low-glare studio depth |
| **Elevated Surface** | `#0F172A` (Deep Slate) | Frosted dark glass panels, brushed anodized charcoal, soft diffused ambient fill |
| **Primary Accent** | `#38BDF8` (Sky Cyan) | Crisp sky-cyan rim light, subtle luminous cyan glass refraction |
| **Secondary Accent** | `#00F5D4` (Mint Teal) | Soft mint-teal volumetric highlight, clean bioluminescent edge accent |
| **Warm Human Balance** | `#F8FAFC` / Natural Daylight | Warm natural key light on human subjects, authentic skin tones, modern sunlit workspace |

---

## 4. Visual Aesthetic & Composition Standards

- **Photography Style:** Authentic editorial photography shot on 35mm or 50mm prime lenses (`f/2.0–f/2.8`), natural shallow depth of field, candid collaboration or crisp macro product detail.
- **3D / Abstract Product Art:** Precision optical glass, subtle refraction, clean geometric composition, generous negative space (`30%–40%` uncluttered area) for headline and logo lockups.
- **Prohibited Visual Elements:**
  - Stock clichés (glowing holographic brains, handshakes in suits, floating binary code matrices).
  - Oversaturated neon cyberpunk clutter or harsh red warning palettes.
  - Distorted hands, unreadable pseudo-text on screens, or third-party brand logos.
